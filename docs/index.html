TNO-FCI: predicting the ISM Manufacturing PMI 10 months ahead

A financial conditions index built from five monthly market and policy series.

Inspired by the financial conditions index of Raoul Pal's Global Macro Investor (GMI), which GMI uses to look about nine months ahead of the ISM PMI (The GMI Top 5 Weekly Charts That Make You Go Hmmm..., August 2023).

TNO-FCI is one number per month, built from the Fed funds rate, the US dollar index, gold, oil and silver. A linear model combines features made from those five series, weighted so that this month's value tracks the ISM Manufacturing PMI 10 months later. The PMI is a monthly survey of US factory activity; above 50 means expansion.

The July 2025 prediction, and what followed

On 26 July 2025 the model, fitted on data through June 2025, predicted the PMI for July 2025 to April 2026. The prediction had the PMI back above 50 from July 2025; the PMI itself crossed 50 in January 2026.

Method

Alongside the five series, the model uses features built from them: their values 3, 6 and 12 months back, momentum, moving averages, volatility and ratios such as gold to silver. The lead is chosen by testing 3 to 18 months and keeping the one where TNO-FCI correlates best with the PMI that many months later; a straight line then maps TNO-FCI to PMI points. In-sample, the fit explains R² 0.46 of the PMI's variation (0.43 in July 2025), so read the prediction as a direction rather than a precise numerical forecast.

Data and code

Monthly series, mostly exported from TradingView: TVC:DXY, TVC:GOLD, TVC:USOIL and TVC:SILVER, plus ECONOMICS:USBCOI (ISM PMI) and ECONOMICS:USINTR (Fed funds rate) through June 2025. From July 2025 the ISM PMI comes from ISM's monthly releases and the Fed funds rate (upper bound of the target range) from FOMC statements. The data isn't redistributed; the code runs on your own copies.
